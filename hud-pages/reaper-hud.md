A red and black cyberpunk HUD: black glass, crimson light and a broken signal, seen through the terminals of a Japanese megacorporation.

- **Main menu:** a looping video of the Wall's circuitry behind the REAPER logo, with its own menu music and startup video.
- **In game:** health and ammo as black glass cards either side of the crosshair, the live class model as an ID photo, red and cyan glitch copies on the digits.
- **Glitches set off by the game:** near death the Blackwall breaks through the screen, the ammo card flickers when low, the über strip flickers READY when full.
- **Team colours:** crimson on RED, azure on BLU, the whole HUD over.
- **Menus:** class select as a code matrix, a split team select, an armoury-style loadout page, backpack, crafting, scoreboard and MvM in the same look.

**HUD Settings** in the main menu: CENTRED or MINMODE layout, 3D model or 2D class art, six crosshairs, Blackwall kill sounds and quick fixes.

Brings its own fonts. Music, videos and sounds from Cyberpunk 2077; a fan-made HUD.
