## Features

- **Competitive captions** — Focused visual alerts for key sound cues, including Medic calls and off-class voicelines.
- **Manual Über timer** — A countdown for estimating the earliest enemy Über and Kritz charge timings.
- **Ambassador crosshair overlay** — A fixed crosshair overlay for the Ambassador.
- **Adjustable viewmodel transparency** — Transparent viewmodels with customisable opacity.
- **Customisable main menu shortcuts** — Personalised shortcut labels and console commands.
