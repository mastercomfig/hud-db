A Bleach / Ulquiorra Cifer themed HUD: Hueco Mundo by night, torn manga pieces in black, bone white and emerald.

- **Main menu:** Hueco Mundo with Las Noches and the ULQUIORRA logo. It changes by itself on full-moon nights, for Scream Fortress and for Smissmas.
- **Menus:** loadout, backpack, scoreboard, class and team select in black, grey and emerald.
- **In game:** your 3D model (or TF2's 2D class art) on a torn triangle with the health beside it, ammo on an ink blade, and ÜberCharge, metal, stickies and item meters as manga cards.
- **Manga-style** kill feed, damage numbers, target ID, death screen, win panel, notifications, Engineer / Spy menus and a hitmarker.

**HUD Settings** in the main menu: CENTERED or CORNERS layout, 3D model or 2D class art.

Uses fonts that come with Windows 10/11 (Bahnschrift, Segoe UI, Constantia).
